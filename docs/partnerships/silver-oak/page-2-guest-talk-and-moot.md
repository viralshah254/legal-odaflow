## Section A: Guest Talk (Primary Ask)

### Title

**Justice for Everyone: How AI Is Reshaping Law in Gujarat and India**

*Alternate titles:*
1. The Future of Legal Practice — AI, Access, and the Next Generation of Lawyers
2. From Courtroom to Code: Building a More Accessible Legal System

### Format

- **Duration:** 45–60 minutes (30–40 min talk + 15–20 min Q&A)
- **Speaker:** Garima Jain, Umbrella by OkayLegal.ai
- **Audience:** LLB / BA LLB students, faculty from law and allied departments, moot court society members
- **Host:** Silver Oak University, Ahmedabad

### Talk Outline (Speaker Notes)

#### 1. Opening — Why we built Umbrella (5 min)

- The gap: millions of Indians need legal help; millions cannot afford timely counsel
- Gujarat context: growing legal services market in Ahmedabad, district courts, Gujarat High Court, startup and SME legal needs
- Our mission: *"Understand your legal problem before you spend thousands on a lawyer"*

#### 2. The state of access to justice in India (8 min)

- Pendency, cost barriers, language barriers, and the "information asymmetry" between citizens and the legal system
- Why law students and young advocates are critical to closing this gap
- Gujarat's role: strong legal education ecosystem (Silver Oak, Gujarat HC bar)

#### 3. How AI is transforming the legal system — responsibly (12 min)

- **Research:** AI that cites real statutes and case law (not generic chatbot answers)
- **Drafting:** Court-ready documents grounded in applicable law — BNS/BNSS, not outdated IPC templates
- **Issue spotting:** Helping citizens identify legal issues before they escalate
- **For lawyers:** Copilot that saves associates hours — not replacing judgment, amplifying it
- **What AI cannot do:** Replace licensed advocates, provide formal legal advice, or appear in court — the human lawyer remains essential

#### 4. Live demo / case walkthrough (8 min)

- Example: A consumer complaint or bail application draft using India Drafts
- Show citation-backed research vs. a generic AI answer
- Optional: FIR analysis or section finder for criminal law students

#### 5. What this means for law students (5 min)

- AI literacy as a core skill for the 2026+ lawyer
- How to use tools ethically (verification, citation checks, client confidentiality)
- Career paths: litigation, in-house, legal tech, policy
- Invitation to explore Umbrella free as a research and drafting companion

#### 6. Q&A (15–20 min)

### What We Need from Silver Oak

- A lecture hall / seminar room (or auditorium)
- 45–60 minutes on a mutually agreed date
- Optional: faculty liaison from the School of Law or moot court committee

### What We Offer

- No cost to the university
- Practical, India-grounded content — not a generic Silicon Valley legal tech talk
- Optional free student access codes for hands-on exploration after the session

## Section B: Moot Court Competition (Follow-Up Proposal)

*Position after the guest session: "We would love to explore a deeper partnership — including co-hosting a moot court competition — after the guest talk."*

### Proposed Event

**Umbrella Moot Court Challenge @ Silver Oak University**

A one-day (or two-day) moot court competition where students argue a contemporary legal problem at the intersection of **technology, access to justice, and constitutional/civil rights** — with Umbrella providing research tools, drafting support, and prizes.

### Why It Fits Silver Oak

- Builds practical skills: research, drafting, oral advocacy
- Connects classroom law to real-world legal tech
- Positions Silver Oak as a forward-looking law program in Gujarat

### Proposed Structure

| Element | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| **Format** | Preliminary written memorials + oral rounds (standard moot format) |
| **Theme options** | (1) Right to legal aid & technology; (2) Data privacy under DPDP Act 2023; (3) AI in judicial decision-making — ethics and limits |
| **Teams** | 2 speakers + 1 researcher per team; open to LLB/BA LLB students |
| **Rounds** | Prelims → Quarters → Semis → Finals |
| **Judging** | Faculty panel + practicing advocates (Umbrella can help invite Gujarat bar members) |

### What Umbrella Provides (Sponsorship)

- Platform access for registered teams during the competition (research + drafting tools)
- Prizes for Best Team, Best Speaker, Best Memorial
- Branding: banners, digital assets, social media coverage
- Guest talk by Garima Jain (can double as opening ceremony keynote)
- Optional: workshop on "AI-Assisted Legal Research" for participants before the competition

### What We Need from Silver Oak

- Venue (moot court hall or auditorium)
- Faculty coordinator / moot court society lead
- Student registration and team management
- Proposed dates (suggest: 4–6 weeks after guest talk to allow planning)
