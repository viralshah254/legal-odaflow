## About Us

**Umbrella by OkayLegal.ai** is India's dual-sided legal AI platform — built so that legal clarity is not reserved for those who can afford expensive counsel.

We help **citizens** understand their legal situation, research applicable law with real citations, and draft court-ready documents — in **Hindi or English** — before they ever step into a lawyer's chamber.

We help **lawyers and law firms** run their entire practice on one AI-native platform: matter management, legal research, document drafting, billing, and client intake — connected in one workspace.

**Our belief:** Legal support should protect everyone, not just a few. Justice shouldn't be complicated. It should be accessible.

## What We Do — Three Pillars

| Pillar | For citizens | For lawyers & students |
|--------|--------------|------------------------|
| **Draft** | Notices, petitions, bail applications, contracts — 394+ India court formats grounded in BNS, BNSS, and BSA 2023 | AI-assisted drafting with jurisdiction-aware templates (including Gujarat-specific formats) |
| **Research** | Issue checker, section finder (IPC/BNS/BNSS), FIR analysis with cited authorities | Verified legal research with statute and case-law citations |
| **Resolve** | Rights checker, risk analysis, and escalation to verified advocates when needed | Matter-aware AI Copilot, strategy memos, and client guidance |

---

## Built for India — Relevant to Gujarat

- **394+ court draft formats** — bail, writ petitions (Art. 226), consumer complaints, cheque bounce (NI Act §138), family petitions, and more
- **New criminal code ready** — grounded in BNS, BNSS, and BSA 2023 (replacing IPC/CrPC)
- **Gujarat-aware** — Gujarat Rent Act notices, registration deed support, and state-level jurisdiction detection
- **Multilingual** — Hindi and English today; designed for India's linguistic diversity
- **Free to start** — students and citizens can explore issue checking and research at no cost
