# Investor Demo Flow

Use seeded demo data to walk through the IPO-quality narrative.

1. Start at consumer issue checker and create an issue.
2. Open firm leads and qualify the lead.
3. Convert or open the demo matter command center.
4. Show matter next-best-actions, AI, documents, evidence, tasks, and meetings.
5. Open documents and show OCR, contract, risk, and bundle readiness.
6. Open finance and show invoice/payment controls.
7. Open the secure client portal and show messages, documents, invoice/payment status, and consent-aware access.
8. Close with Privacy & Security, AI Command Center, and `/health/readiness`.

Seeded accounts use development-only credentials and must be rotated before production demos.
