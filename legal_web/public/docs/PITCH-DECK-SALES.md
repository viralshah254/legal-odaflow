## Slide 10 — Security & Compliance

**Built for the ethical obligations of legal practice — not bolted on.**

**Access Control**
- Role-based access control (RBAC) — every user sees only what they should
- Ethical walls — restrict specific matters from specific firm users, enforced in AI retrieval and document access
- Matter-level permissions — clients and external counsel see only their matter

**AI Governance**
- Training consent denied by default — your client data is never used to train AI models without explicit opt-in
- Every AI output is logged with the prompt, model, and output — full audit trail
- AI citations are verified against retrieved sources — no invented legal authorities
- Lawyer approval required before any AI output is sent to a client or filed externally

**Audit & Compliance**
- Comprehensive audit log for every document access, matter change, and billing event
- SAML/SSO support for enterprise firms
- SOC 2 path in progress

**Your client data belongs to your firm. Full stop.**

> Note: This slide closes deals with managing partners who were burned by consumer AI tools. The training consent default and audit log are differentiators — most competitors require opt-out, not opt-in.
