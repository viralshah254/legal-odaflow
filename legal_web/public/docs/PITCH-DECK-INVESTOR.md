## Slide 3 — The Problem

Legal help is broken on **both sides** — and neither side knows the other exists.

**Consumers**
- Don't know if they have a legal problem, what law applies, or what to do next
- Google blindly, delay, or hire too late — after deadlines and leverage are gone
- Billions have unmet legal needs they never act on

**Law firms**
- 40–60% of time on admin: intake, research, documents, billing, updates
- Fragmented tools — PM here, billing there, AI chat with no matter context
- No connection between how clients arrive and how work gets done

**One line:** People lack legal knowledge. Lawyers lack time. The industry runs on friction.

---

## Slide 4 — The Insight

Legal tech spent 20 years solving **half** the problem.

| Category | Examples | Where it stops |
|----------|----------|----------------|
| Firm software | Clio, MyCase, Smokeball | No consumer funnel |
| Legal AI | Harvey, CoCounsel | No billing, matters, or compliance |
| Consumer forms | LegalZoom, Rocket Lawyer | Shallow analysis; dead-end at checkout |

**Umbrella closes the loop end-to-end** — consumer entry, lawyer validation, firm operations, outcome data — one stack, one data layer.

## Slide 14 — Go-to-Market

**Phase 1 — India + US (now)**
- Consumer: SEO, free issue checker, mobile app
- Firms: Solo and small-mid (5–50 lawyers) upgrading from fragmented tools
- Marketplace: Verified lawyers in family, property, employment, consumer disputes

**Phase 2 — Supply density**
- Bar associations, legal aid, word-of-mouth from consumer → lawyer handoffs
- Product-led growth: marketplace leads convert firms to OS customers

**Phase 3 — Kenya, UK, enterprise**
- Multi-jurisdiction plans, SSO, ethical walls, migration tooling

**GTM is asymmetric:** Consumer = organic/SEO (low CAC). Firms = outbound + product-led from marketplace leads.
