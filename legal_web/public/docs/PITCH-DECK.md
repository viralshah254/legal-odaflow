## Slide 2 — The Problem (short)

Legal help is broken on both sides of the market.

**For individuals**
- Most people do not know if they have a legal problem, what law applies, or what to do next.
- They either ignore the issue, Google blindly, or hire a lawyer too late — after time limits, evidence, or leverage are gone.

**For law firms**
- Lawyers spend 40–60% of their time on admin: intake, research, documents, billing, updates, and client communication — not on legal judgment.
- Tools are fragmented: one app for matters, another for billing, another for AI chat — none connected to how clients actually arrive.

**One sentence:** People lack legal knowledge; lawyers lack time. The industry runs on friction.

---

## Slide 3 — What We Are Actually Solving

We are not building “another legal chatbot” or “another Clio clone.”

**The real problem:** Legal access and legal operations are disconnected. Consumers enter the system confused. Firms enter with no context. Nothing flows from “I have a problem” to “this is a matter, billed, researched, and resolved.”

**Umbrella connects the full loop:**

```
Individual describes issue → AI legal clarity → optional lawyer review → matter opens in firm OS → outcome tracked
```

That closed loop does not exist in legal tech today.

## Slide 5 — Solution

**Umbrella is three products, one platform:**

1. **Consumer Legal Guidance** — Issue checker, jurisdiction-aware analysis, 15-section risk reports, legal forms, document upload.
2. **AI-Native Lawyer OS** — Matters, billing, trust, time, DMS, meetings → transcript → timeline, Copilot, automations, client portal.
3. **Marketplace Bridge** — Verified lawyers receive warm, pre-analyzed leads — not cold form fills.

**North star:** Help anyone understand their legal situation and escalate to a verified lawyer when needed — while giving lawyers the OS to run and grow their entire practice.

Legal tech has evolved in silos. **The integration layer was never built.**

| Era | What shipped | What’s missing |

| **Umbrella** | **Dual-sided, AI-native, outcome-governed** | **Consumer → marketplace → matter → analytics — one stack** |

**Innovations that don’t exist elsewhere (combined):**
