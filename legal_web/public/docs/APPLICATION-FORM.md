# Umbrella — Application Form Responses

> Dual-sided AI legal platform. **Launching in India first.** Built for the world.

---

## Problem Statement

Every day, someone loses a home, a job, custody of a child, or years of savings—not because the law wasn’t on their side, but because they never knew they had rights in the first place.

This is not an edge case. It is the reality for hundreds of millions of people across India and the world. Legal justice feels reserved for the wealthy, the connected, the ones who already know how to speak the language of courts and contracts. Everyone else waits until it’s too late—until a deadline passes, evidence disappears, or the other side moves first.

Meanwhile, the lawyers who *could* help are drowning. Not in cases—in paperwork. Intake forms. Billing spreadsheets. Research rabbit holes. Tools that don’t talk to each other. Up to 60% of a lawyer’s week vanishes into admin, not justice.

Two broken worlds. One missing bridge. And millions of lives caught in the gap between them.

---

## Target Audience

**The people the system forgot.**

The tenant facing an illegal eviction who doesn’t know where to file a complaint. The worker cheated out of wages who assumes fighting back costs more than staying silent. The parent in a custody battle who has no idea what the next step even is. The small-business owner buried under a contract they never fully understood.

These are not “users.” They are mothers, fathers, workers, entrepreneurs—millions in India first, and billions globally—who deserve clarity before they deserve a courtroom.

**And the lawyers who want to help—but can’t get to the work that matters.**

Solo advocates and growing firms losing precious hours every week to intake chaos, manual research, billing reconciliation, and software that was never built to work together. They entered law to fight for people. Instead, they fight their inbox.

We start in India because the need is urgent and the scale is unmatched. We build for the world because injustice doesn’t stop at a border.

---

## Your Solution

**Umbrella** gives people their voice back—and gives lawyers their time back.

For someone who has never set foot in a lawyer’s office: describe what’s happening in your own words. No jargon. No judgment. Our AI reads the actual law—real statutes, real cases—and tells you where you stand, what your options are, and what to do next. Free to start. Private. Never used to train AI without your consent.

When the moment is serious enough, one tap connects you to a verified lawyer who already understands your situation—not a cold form, not a blank slate.

For the firm on the other side: one AI-native operating system for matters, billing, documents, research, client communication, and intake—replacing the patchwork of tools that steals your day. Warm leads arrive pre-analyzed. A matter opens in one click. The loop closes.

From confusion to clarity. From lead to resolution. One platform. India first. For everyone, everywhere.

---

## Innovation / Differentiation

The legal industry has spent decades building half-solutions.

Chatbots that sound smart but cite nothing real. Practice software that never meets the person who needs help. Consumer forms that end the moment you pay—leaving you alone with a PDF and a prayer.

**Umbrella is the first platform that connects the entire journey:**

A person in rural India understanding their rights → a verified lawyer reviewing their case → a matter opening in a firm’s operating system → an outcome tracked with care.

**What makes us different:**

- **Truth over performance.** AI grounded in cited law—not confident guesses.
- **The closed loop.** Consumer clarity, lawyer validation, firm operations, and outcome data in one stack—not four vendors and a prayer.
- **Built for India, ready for the world.** Local law, local languages, local payments—architected multi-country from day one.
- **Privacy as a promise.** Your story stays yours. Training consent denied by default.
- **Time returned to justice.** Meeting capture, research, billing, and client portal unified—so lawyers do what they trained for.

We’re not building another tool. We’re building the bridge millions have been waiting to cross.

## What is your vision?

A world where understanding your legal rights is as basic as knowing how to read—and where no one has to choose between justice and rent.

We envision a future where a farmer in Uttar Pradesh, a gig worker in Bangalore, and a small-business owner in Mumbai can describe what’s happening to them—in their language, on their phone—and receive honest, cited guidance before fear or cost shuts them down. Where the first conversation with a lawyer begins with clarity, not confusion. Where escalation to a professional is a choice made from strength, not desperation.

For lawyers, we envision a profession reclaimed: less inbox, more impact. Firms powered by AI that handles the admin so humans handle the judgment. Advocates spending their days fighting for people—not fighting spreadsheets.

We launch in India because that is where the need is deepest and the opportunity to change lives is greatest. But our vision does not stop at a border. Umbrella becomes the infrastructure layer for legal access everywhere—connecting every person who needs help to every lawyer ready to give it, until the gap between knowing your rights and exercising them finally closes.

**Our north star:** Legal intelligence for anyone. Anywhere. With dignity.
