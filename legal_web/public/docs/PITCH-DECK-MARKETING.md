## Slide 8 — When You Need a Real Lawyer, We Connect You

**Umbrella is honest about when AI is not enough.**

Some situations need a licensed lawyer. When yours does, Umbrella connects you to a verified professional who already knows your situation.

**How it works:**
- Your report includes a recommendation if a lawyer is needed
- You request a lawyer review directly from your report
- A verified lawyer in your jurisdiction receives your full context — issue type, documents, AI analysis
- They review your situation and provide their professional assessment
- You pay a clear, upfront fee — no surprise hourly billing

**The difference from calling a lawyer cold:**
- The lawyer already has your context — no retelling the whole story
- You know what to expect before the call
- The review is focused and efficient — you pay for judgment, not explanation

**Verified lawyers in India, US, Kenya, and United Kingdom.**

> Note: This is the high-trust, high-value slide. The message is: Umbrella is not trying to replace lawyers. It's making the path to a lawyer clearer, faster, and less intimidating. This reduces the objection "but I need real legal advice."

## Slide 10 — Safe, Private, and Honest

**We tell you what the law says. We do not pretend to be your lawyer.**

**What Umbrella will always tell you:**
- Umbrella provides legal information and risk analysis — not legal advice
- For advice specific to your situation, consult a qualified lawyer in your jurisdiction
- Every AI output includes this disclaimer, on every report, every time

**What Umbrella will never do:**
- Claim to guarantee a legal outcome
- Invent a law or court case that does not exist
- Share your information with third parties without your consent
- Use your data to train AI models without your explicit permission

**Your privacy:**
- Your legal issues are private — we treat them that way
- Documents you upload are stored securely and accessible only to you
- You control who sees your information — including any lawyer you contact through the platform

**We are honest about what AI can and cannot do. That is what makes us trustworthy.**

> Note: This slide handles the objection before it is raised. Legal AI has a trust problem — people worry about bad advice, data leaks, and hallucinated laws. Address it directly. The training consent clause is a genuine differentiator.
