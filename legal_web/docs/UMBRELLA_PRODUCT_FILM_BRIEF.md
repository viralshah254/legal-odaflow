# Umbrella product film brief

## Purpose
Create a 45–60 second original product film showing scattered legal work becoming one connected Umbrella workspace. The film should feel calm, precise, human, and premium—not like an AI demo montage.

| Time | Scene | Camera and transition | On-screen text | Required assets |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0–5s | Documents, messages, research tabs and deadlines appear disconnected. | Slow macro drift through layered paper and interface planes. | Legal work is scattered. | Abstract documents, message fragments, calendar cards |
| 5–10s | Umbrella canopy arcs form and draw the layers inward. | Pull back into the glowing canopy core. | Bring it under one Umbrella. | Canopy/rib model, data-flow trails |
| 10–20s | A plain-language question resolves into a cited research memo. | Push through a query field into authorities and highlighted passages. | Research that shows its work. | Research UI board, citation cards, source excerpts |
| 20–30s | Matter facts form an outline and first draft. | Lateral sweep from facts into a structured document. | From facts to a first draft. | Matter facts, document outline, editable draft |
| 30–38s | A long contract is reviewed; important terms rise to the surface. | Overhead document scan with restrained focus pulls. | Clear decisions from long documents. | Review UI, risk/highlight layers |
| 38–46s | Research, documents, tasks and client details collect around one matter. | Wide orbit around a connected workspace. | One connected matter. | Matter dashboard, tasks, calendar, client panel |
| 46–54s | Individual guidance passes into a lawyer/team workspace. | Two visual worlds join across a light path. | From question to professional help. | Consumer report, handoff panel, team workspace |
| 54–60s | Canopy returns in a calm final state with logo and product UI. | Settle into a still, protected composition. | Your legal work. Under one Umbrella. | Final canopy, Umbrella logo, interface frame |

## Direction
- Use an abstract protective canopy, not a literal umbrella prop.
- Keep camera movement deliberate: slow drift, pull-back, and one measured orbit.
- Use soft navy, Umbrella blue, paper white, and quiet document-grey; avoid generic neon or purple gradients.
- Keep UI legible but do not rely on the film for critical page information.

## Sound and voiceover
- Sound: soft paper movement, restrained interface ticks, a low protective tonal bed, and one resolving swell at the end.
- Voiceover draft: “Legal work rarely starts in one place. Umbrella brings the question, the research, the documents and the people around a matter into one connected workspace. Your legal work. Under one Umbrella.”

## Deliverables
- 16:9 desktop master, 9:16 mobile cut, and a silent 8–12 second website loop.
- WebM and MP4 website exports, poster-frame WebP, caption VTT, and alpha-background canopy exports where suitable.
- Figma UI boards for every product shot; Blender scene files for canopy/document environments; After Effects project for compositing and typography.
